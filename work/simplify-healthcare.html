01 · Context

Training customers on complex software.

The training function served employees of healthcare-client organizations who needed to learn software their organization had purchased. The work sat between customer education, learning technology, instructional design, product readiness and customer enablement.

02 · Operating model
  • LMS ownershipI selected and procured Docebo for the customer-training use case and built/configured the learning environment around client needs.
  • People leadershipI directly managed two trainers: hiring, goals, scheduling, coaching, work assignment and content quality.
  • DeliveryI delivered live customer training and helped move the program from onsite delivery to primarily remote learning during COVID.
  • Release readinessI participated in UAT and scrum-related work so training stayed aligned with product changes.
  • Operational reportingI reported attendance, completions, passes/certificates, learner scores and feedback, readiness, and team workload to leadership.

The strongest part of the work was the system around the learning: platform, people, product changes, delivery and customer readiness.
