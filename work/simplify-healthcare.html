Customer education

Simplify Healthcare

Building and operating a customer-training system for healthcare software users.

Role: Training Supervisor / Instructional DesignerAudience: Client employeesScale: ~20 client organizations · ~500 learners · ~100–300 assets

Training customers on complex software

The training function served employees of healthcare-client organizations who needed to learn software their organization had purchased. The work therefore sat between customer education, learning technology, instructional design, product readiness and customer enablement.

The operating model

I directly managed two trainers, coordinated scheduling and delivery quality, partnered with an SME and product stakeholders, and maintained the systems around customer learning.

~20 client organizations · ~500 learners · ~100–300 assetsA customer-training operation rather than a single course project.

LMS ownership and platform decisions

I selected and procured Docebo for the customer-training use case and built/configured the learning environment around client needs. The organization later moved to Adobe Captivate Prime / Adobe Learning Manager through a migration handled separately.

The work included users, courses, delivery structures, reporting, certification and ongoing content operations.

Content, delivery and release readiness

The learning library included courses, videos and supporting assets translating software workflows into usable training. I delivered live customer training roughly twice per month, helped transition delivery from onsite sessions to primarily remote learning during COVID, and participated in release/UAT and scrum-related work so training stayed aligned with product changes.

Leadership responsibilities

Direct people management included hiring, goals, scheduling, time off, work assignment, coaching, performance issues and content-quality review. I also reported attendance, completions, certificates, learner scores/feedback, readiness and team workload to leadership.

What this demonstrates

Customer education · SaaS/software training · LMS selection and ownership · people management · instructional design · UAT/release readiness · virtual delivery · stakeholder collaboration.

The strongest part of the case is the system around the learning: platform, people, product changes, delivery, quality and customer readiness.
