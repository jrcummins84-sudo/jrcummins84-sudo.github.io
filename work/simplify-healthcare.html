Customer education

Simplify Healthcare

Building and operating a customer-training system for healthcare software users.

Role: Training Supervisor / Instructional DesignerAudience: Client employeesScale: ~500 learners over tenure

Training customers on complex software

The training function served employees of client organizations who needed to learn the healthcare software their organization had purchased. The work therefore sat between customer education, learning technology, instructional design and product enablement.

The operating model

Product and subject-matter expertise flowed into a training team, a customer-learning platform and structured delivery. Josh supervised trainers, coordinated calendars and quality, worked with SMEs and product teams, and supported certification and learner readiness.

~500 learners · 100–300 courses, videos and assetsA customer-training operation rather than a single course project.

Learning systems and content

Josh created and managed the customer-training LMS environment. The program used Adobe Captivate Prime / Adobe Learning Manager and later Docebo. Josh selected Docebo for the customer-training use case; the technical migration itself was handled separately.

The content library included software instruction, videos and supporting assets built to translate complex workflows into teachable experiences.

What this demonstrates

Customer education · LMS ownership · instructional design · trainer leadership · stakeholder collaboration · content operations.

The work shows the ability to run the system around learning—not just create the learning itself.
