01 · Context

A learning system, not a course repository.

ECHO Academy has to support different technical experience levels, business roles, organizational structures, languages, certification requirements and delivery formats while remaining understandable to learners and operable for the organization.

My work spans platform administration, learning architecture, learner segmentation, reporting, certification, multilingual content operations, blended learning and learner experience.

03 · Programs

Some programs reach nearly the entire active learner population.

At the October 2026 snapshot, Required Annual Training had 16,828 enrolled learners, TF1 Certification had 16,660, and Sales Update Certification had 16,006.

Multilingual operations span English, French, Spanish and Portuguese. The Spanish troubleshooting learning plan alone showed 11,445 enrollments.

The strongest learning-system work is often invisible to the learner: structure, segmentation, reporting logic, governance and maintenance.

04 · Reporting

Reporting is infrastructure.

Custom reports combine learner attributes, organizational hierarchy, course and learning-plan status, completion dates and dealer/service metadata to answer operational questions—not simply produce exports.

Metrics are dated snapshots, not lifetime totals. Public screenshots intentionally omit learner-list PII.
