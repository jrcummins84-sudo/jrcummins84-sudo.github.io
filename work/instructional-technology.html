From classroom to systems

Josh’s work moved from English teaching into instructional technology, teacher coaching, district planning, implementation, professional development and technology support. Teaching adults became an increasingly important part of the work.

North Chicago: 1:1 implementation

The Chromebook initiative involved the full lifecycle: top-level district planning, implementation, teacher training, technology support, and planning and training for professional-development and workday conferences.

Technology adoption is a learning problem.Devices matter only when infrastructure, teacher learning, support and instructional practice reinforce one another.

District 300

A public 2014–2015 District 300 staff roster independently documents Josh at the district offices as an instructional technology facilitator. The broader remembered role is best described as Instructional Technology Facilitator / Coach.

The exact start and end dates remain under historical reconstruction, so the portfolio intentionally avoids overstating the chronology.

What this demonstrates

Implementation · adult learning · technology coaching · support · professional development · systems thinking.

These patterns later carried directly into corporate training, LMS administration and learning-technology leadership.
