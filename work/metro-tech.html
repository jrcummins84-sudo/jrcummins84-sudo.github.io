Operations → training

Metro Tech

Turning firsthand operational knowledge into a headquarters onboarding and training function.

Progression: Dispatch → coordination / management → trainingLeadership: ~12 dispatchers

Learn the operation first

Josh began in dispatch and grew into broader coordination, management and training responsibilities across nationwide HVAC/service operations. That progression mattered because the training was built by someone who had already lived the workflows it needed to explain.

The training challenge

New hires needed to understand company software, service workflows, operational standards and the 1:1 technology used by employees and field staff. Training had to connect tools, process and judgment rather than teach them as isolated topics.

Building the function

Metro Tech flew new employees to Schaumburg for headquarters training that Josh built and ran. The program included structured onboarding, company-software instruction, process training and reusable video content covering software, service workflows and operational topics.

From frontline work to repeatable knowledge transferOperational experience became management knowledge, then a training system.

What this demonstrates

Operations knowledge · team leadership · training-function build · technical enablement · video/content development · process improvement.
