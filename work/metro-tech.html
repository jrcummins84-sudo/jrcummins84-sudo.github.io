Operations → training

Metro Tech

Turning firsthand operational knowledge into a headquarters onboarding and training function for nationwide service operations.

Progression: Call dispatch → coordination / management → trainingLeadership: ~12 dispatchersScope: Nationwide HVAC/service operations

Learn the operation first

I started as a dispatcher and advanced into broader coordination, management and training responsibilities. That progression mattered because the training was built by someone who had already lived the workflows it needed to explain.

The training challenge

New hires had to understand company software, service workflows, operational standards, national-account expectations and the 1:1 technology used by employees and field staff. Training had to connect tools, process and judgment rather than teach them as isolated topics.

Building the function

Metro Tech flew new employees to Schaumburg for headquarters training that I built and ran. The program included structured onboarding, company-software instruction, process training and reusable video content.

Training videos covered software/process tutorials, HVAC/service workflows and onboarding topics. LMS use was limited; the major accomplishment was building the training function and repeatable onboarding system itself.

From frontline work to repeatable knowledge transferOperational experience became management knowledge, then a training system.

Operations leadership

Alongside training, I managed approximately 12 dispatchers and worked across technicians, national accounts, scheduling, service quality, audits, customer issues and regional staff. That operational responsibility kept training tied to real work rather than abstract procedure.

What this demonstrates

Operations knowledge · people leadership · training-function build · technical enablement · video/content development · process improvement · onboarding.

The case shows a repeated pattern in my career: understand the workflow deeply enough to teach it, then formalize it into a system that scales.
