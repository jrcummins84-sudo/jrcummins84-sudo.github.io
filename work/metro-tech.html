02 · Training function

Operational knowledge became reusable training.

New hires traveled to Schaumburg for structured onboarding that I built and ran. The program included company-software instruction, process training, device/technology use and reusable video content covering software and service workflows.

The major accomplishment was not an LMS implementation. It was building a repeatable training function out of real operational knowledge.

Learn the operation deeply enough to teach it, then formalize it into a system that scales.
