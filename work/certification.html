Technical Certification

01 · Problem

Progression without unnecessary friction.

Newer technicians need structured learning. Experienced technicians may already possess the knowledge the standard pathway is designed to teach. The architecture has to support both without weakening the certification.

02 · Pathways
  • TF1Foundational certification with both a multi-course learning route and a bypass assessment.
  • TF2Advanced certification with audience-specific progression.
  • MasterFinal-stage certification representing the highest level in the structured pathway.
