Technology adoption is a learning problem

The transition from English teaching into instructional technology expanded my focus from one classroom to the systems around many classrooms: device planning, implementation, teacher learning, support, professional development and instructional practice.

North Chicago: full lifecycle implementation

My role spanned top-level planning, device rollout, teacher training, classroom coaching, troubleshooting, Google Workspace-related setup and policy work, and professional-development/workday conference planning.

The device is only one part of adoption.Infrastructure, teacher confidence, support and instructional practice have to reinforce one another.
Planning

Support a district initiative before devices reach classrooms.

Professional development

Train educators to use technology in ways connected to actual teaching practice.

Classroom coaching

Work alongside teachers as they integrate new tools into instruction.

Support

Troubleshoot the practical issues that determine whether adoption succeeds.

Change management

Help educators move from unfamiliar technology toward repeatable classroom use.

Systems thinking

Connect devices, accounts, policy, pedagogy and support rather than treating them as separate projects.
