Learn the operation first

I started in dispatch and advanced into broader coordination, management and training responsibilities. That progression gave me direct experience with scheduling, national accounts, field technicians, service quality, audits, customer issues and the software that tied the operation together.

The training function was built from inside the operation rather than imposed on it from outside.

The training challenge

New employees needed to understand company software, operational expectations, HVAC/service workflows and the 1:1 devices used by employees and field staff. The job was not simply teaching buttons—it was connecting technology, process and judgment.

Make tacit operational knowledge teachable.What experienced people knew from daily work had to become a repeatable onboarding experience.
Headquarters onboarding

Employees were flown to Schaumburg for structured new-hire training that I built and ran.
