Customer education, not internal training

The audience was employees of healthcare clients learning enterprise software their organizations had purchased. Training therefore had to support software adoption, role readiness and customer confidence—not simply course completion.

Building the operating system around training

I created and managed the customer-training LMS environment, selected Docebo for the customer-training use case, supervised two trainers, coordinated schedules, reviewed work, collaborated with SMEs and product teams, and maintained the quality of a growing learning library.

The program later used Adobe Captivate Prime / Adobe Learning Manager. The platform migration itself was handled separately, so the case study distinguishes platform selection and learning operations from technical migration ownership.

LMS ownership

Configure users, courses, delivery structures, certification and reporting for external learners.

Trainer leadership

Hiring, goals, scheduling, coaching, assignments, reviews and content-quality feedback.

Content operations

Maintain a substantial library of software instruction, videos and supporting learning assets.

Product readiness

Participate in release/UAT work and update training as software behavior changes.

Live delivery

Deliver customer training and help move delivery from onsite sessions toward remote delivery during COVID.

Stakeholder reporting

Communicate attendance, completion, certificates, scores, feedback, readiness and team workload.

What this demonstrates

Customer education · SaaS enablement · LMS ownership · people leadership · instructional design · product release readiness · UAT · content operations · virtual delivery.

The strongest part of this work is the system around the learning: people, platform, product knowledge, customer schedules, content quality and readiness all had to stay aligned.
