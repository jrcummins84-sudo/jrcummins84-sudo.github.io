About

Learning systems, explained and improved.

My career has moved through English teaching, instructional technology, training leadership, instructional design and LMS administration. The common thread is learning how complicated systems work, then making them clearer, more useful and easier to operate.

How I work

I tend to work at the intersection of disciplines that organizations often separate: platform administration, instructional design, analytics, content operations, learner experience and technical communication.

That matters because a learning platform is not just software. Course quality, audience structure, reporting, navigation, certification logic and content governance all affect whether learners can find and use what they need.

I have more than a decade of LMS administration experience across platforms including Docebo, Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Lessonly and Litmos, and more than a decade of training-content creation experience using tools including Articulate, Camtasia, Adobe Captivate, Vyond, PowerPoint and related media workflows.

What I’m interested in next

I’m especially interested in senior learning-technology, learning-systems and AI-enabled learning roles where I can combine systems thinking, product judgment, instructional design, analytics and creative problem-solving.

Creative practice

Outside learning technology, I’m a wildlife and macro photographer. Photography reinforces the same habits that show up in my systems work: close observation, patience, composition, and making complexity visible.
